Positive solutions of boundary value problems with nonlinear nonlocal boundary conditions

Abstract:

We consider the existence of at least three positive solutions of a nonlinear first order problem with a nonlinear nonlocal boundary condition given by x'(t) = r(t)x(t) + ∑_{j=1}^{m} f_{j}(t,x(t)), t ∈ [0,1], λ x(0) = x(1) + ∑_{j=1}^{m} λ_{j}(τ_{j},x(τ_{j})), τ_{j} ∈ [0,1], where r: [0,1] → [0,∞) is continuous; the nonlocal points satisfy 0 ≤ τ_{1} < τ_{2} < … < τ_{n} ≤ 1, the nonlinear function f_{i} and τ_{j} are continuous mappings from [0,1] × [0,∞) → [0,∞) for i = 1, 2, …, m and j = 1, 2, …, n respectively, and λ > 0 is a positive parameter.

Keywords:

[positive solutions, Leggett-Williams fixed point theorem, nonlinear boundary conditions]
