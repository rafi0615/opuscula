On the existence of solutions for a class of nonlinear fractional differential equations with integral boundary conditions

Abstract:

In this paper, we study the existence of solutions for a class of nonlinear fractional differential equations with integral boundary conditions. By using the coincidence degree theory, we obtain some new existence results. The main results are well illustrated with an example.

Keywords:

[fractional differential equation, integral boundary condition, coincidence degree theory, existence of solutions]
