A note on the existence of positive solutions for a class of singular fractional boundary value problems

Abstract:

In this paper, we study the existence of positive solutions for a class of singular fractional boundary value problems. By using a fixed point theorem on cones, we establish some new existence results. Our nonlinearity may be singular in both its time and space variables.

Keywords:

[fractional differential equation, singular boundary value problem, positive solution, fixed point theorem]
