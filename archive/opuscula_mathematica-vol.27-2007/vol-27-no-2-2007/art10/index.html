Algebraic construction of a coboundary of a given cycle

Abstract:

We present an algebraic construction of the coboundary of a given cycle as a simpler alternative to the geometric one introduced in [M. Allili, T. Kaczyfiski, Geometric construction of a coboundary of a cycle, Discrete Comput. Geom. 25 (2001), 125–140, T. Kaczyfiski, Recursive coboundary formula for cycles in acyclic chain complexes, Topol. Methods Nonlinear Anal. 18 (2001), 351–371].

Keywords:

[algorithm, homology theory, cycle, coboundary.]
