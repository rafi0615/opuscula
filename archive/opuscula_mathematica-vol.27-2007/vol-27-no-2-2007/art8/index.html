On the equivalence of pre-Schroder equations

Abstract:

In the paper the equivalence of the system of two pre-Schroder functional equations (equations \((S_n)\), \((S_m)\) for \(m>n\geq 3\), \(n,m\in\mathbb{N}\)) and the whole system \((S)\), is considered. The results solve the problem of Gy. Targonski [Gy. Targonski, Problem P 63, Aequationes Math. 4 (1970), 251] in a particular case.
