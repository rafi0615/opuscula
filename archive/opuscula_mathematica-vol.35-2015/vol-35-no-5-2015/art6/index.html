Maillet type theorem for singular first order nonlinear partial differential equations of totally characteristic type. Part II

Abstract:

In this paper, we study the following nonlinear first order partial differential equation: \( f(t,x,u,\partial_{t}u,\partial_{x}u) = 0 \) with \( u(0,x) = 0 \). The purpose of this paper is to determine the estimate of Gevrey order under the condition that the equation is singular of a totally characteristic type. The Gevrey order is indicated by the rate of divergence of a formal power series. This paper is a continuation of the previous papers [Convergence of formal solutions of singular first order nonlinear partial differential equations of totally characteristic type, Funkcial. Ekvac. 45 (2002), 187-208] and [Maillet type theorem for singular first order nonlinear partial differential equations of totally characteristic type, Surikaiseki Kenkyujo Kokyuroku, Kyoto University 1431 (2005), 94-106]. Especially the last-mentioned paper is regarded as part I of this paper.
