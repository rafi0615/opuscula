On the existence of solutions of the Cauchy problem for the nonlinear wave equation with small data in the Gevrey class

Abstract:

We consider the Cauchy problem for the nonlinear wave equation with small initial data. We prove that if the nonlinear term is of the derivative type, then the Cauchy problem has a unique solution in the Gevrey class.

Keywords:

[nonlinear wave equation, Cauchy problem, Gevrey class, small initial data]
