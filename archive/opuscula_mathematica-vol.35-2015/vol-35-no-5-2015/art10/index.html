On the Cauchy problem for non-effectively hyperbolic operators with multiple characteristics

Abstract:

We study the Cauchy problem for non-effectively hyperbolic operators with multiple characteristics. We prove that the Cauchy problem is well-posed in the Gevrey class of order \( s < k \) if the Hamilton map has a non-zero real eigenvalue at the multiple characteristic points of order \( k \).
