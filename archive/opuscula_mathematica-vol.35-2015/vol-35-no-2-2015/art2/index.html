Simple eigenvectors of unbounded operators of the type 'normal plus compact'

Abstract:

The paper deals with operators of the form \(A=S+B\), where \(B\) is a compact operator in a Hilbert space \(H\) and \(S\) is an unbounded normal one in \(H\), having a compact resolvent. We consider approximations of the eigenvectors of \(A\), corresponding to simple eigenvalues by the eigenvectors of the operators \(A_{n}=S+B_{n}\) (\(n=1,2,\ldots\)), where \(B_{n}\) is an \(n\)-dimensional operator. In addition, we obtain the error estimate of the approximation.
