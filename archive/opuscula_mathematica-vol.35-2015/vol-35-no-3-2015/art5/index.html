Spectra of some selfadjoint Jacobi operators in the double root case

Abstract:

In this paper we prove a mixed spectrum of Jacobi operators defined by λₙ = s(n)(1 + x(n)) and qₙ = -2s(n)(1 + y(n)), where (s(n)) is a real unbounded sequence, (x(n)) and (y(n)) are some perturbations.

Keywords:

[Jacobi matrices, double root case, asymptotic behavior, subordination theory, absolutely continuous spectrum, discrete spectrum]
