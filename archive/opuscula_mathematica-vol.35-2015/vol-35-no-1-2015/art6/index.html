A note on the existence of positive solutions for a class of singular semipositone systems

Abstract:

We study the existence of positive solutions for the system of second order differential equations $u'' + f(t,u,v) = 0$, $v'' + g(t,u,v) = 0$ in $(0,1)$ with the boundary conditions $u(0) = u(1) = 0$, $v(0) = v(1) = 0$, where the nonlinearities $f$ and $g$ are continuous, may change sign and may be singular at $t = 0$ and $t = 1$. We prove an existence result via a nonlinear alternative of Leray-Schauder type and illustrate it with an example.
