On the existence of solutions of a second order discrete boundary value problem

Abstract:

We study the existence and multiplicity of positive solutions for the system of second order difference equations $\Delta^2 u_{i-1} + f_i(n, u_n, v_n) = 0$, $n \in \{1, ..., N-1\}$, $i = 1,2$, with the boundary conditions $u_0 = 0$, $u_N = 0$, $v_0 = 0$, $v_N = 0$, where $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $N \geq 2$, and $f_1$, $f_2$ are sign-changing nonlinearities. The proof is based on the Guo-Krasnosel'skii fixed point theorem.
