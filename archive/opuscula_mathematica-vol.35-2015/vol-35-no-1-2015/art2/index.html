Asymptotic behavior of positive solutions of a semilinear Dirichlet problem in the annulus

Abstract:

In this paper, we establish existence and asymptotic behavior of a positive classical solution to the following semilinear boundary value problem: $-\Delta u = q(x)u^\sigma$ in $\Omega$, $u_{|\partial\Omega} = 0$. Here $\Omega$ is an annulus in $R^n$, $n \geq 3$, $\sigma < 1$ and $q$ is a positive function in $C^{r}_{loc}(\Omega)$, $0 < r < 1$, satisfying some appropriate assumptions related to Karamata regular variation theory. Our arguments combine a method of sub- and supersolutions with Karamata regular variation theory.
