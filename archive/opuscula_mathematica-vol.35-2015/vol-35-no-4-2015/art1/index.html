On dynamical systems induced by p-adic number fields

Abstract:

In this paper, we construct dynamical systems induced by p-adic number fields Q_p. We study the corresponding crossed product operator algebras induced by such dynamical systems. In particular, we are interested in structure theorems, and free distributional data of elements in the operator algebras.

Keywords:

[prime fields, p-adic number fields, the Adele ring, p-adic von Neumann algebras, p-adic dynamical systems]
