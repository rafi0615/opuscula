Notes on the nonlinear dependence of a multiscale coupled system with respect to the interface

Abstract:

This work studies the dependence of the solution on interface geometric perturbations in a multiscaled coupled Darcy flow system. A set of admissible perturbations and convergence sense is presented. Sufficient conditions for strong convergence are given. The rate of convergence is fully solved in 1D using orthogonal decompositions and analyzed in simple multi-D settings via nonlinear operators induced by geometric perturbations.

Keywords:

[multiscale coupled systems, interface geometric perturbations, variational formulations, nonlinear dependence]
