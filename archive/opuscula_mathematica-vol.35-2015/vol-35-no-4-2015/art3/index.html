On potential kernels associated with random dynamical systems

Abstract:

Let (θ,φ) be a continuous random dynamical system. The associated potential kernel V is given by Vf(ω, x) = ∫_0^∞ f(θ_t ω, φ(t, ω)x) dt. We prove the equivalence between the properness of the potential kernel and the existence of a global Lyapunov function. This generalizes a known deterministic result and provides a constructive method for Lyapunov functions in gradient-like random systems.
