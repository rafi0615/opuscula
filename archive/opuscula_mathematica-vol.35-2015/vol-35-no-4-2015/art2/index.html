Oscillation criteria for third order nonlinear delay differential equations with damping

Abstract:

This note is concerned with the oscillation of third order nonlinear delay differential equations of the form (r2(t)(r1(t)y'(t)))' + p(t)y'(t) + q(t)f(y(q(t))) = 0. We improve and unify results from previous papers and present new sufficient conditions for oscillation or convergence to zero of solutions, considering both cases when an associated second order equation is nonoscillatory or oscillatory.
