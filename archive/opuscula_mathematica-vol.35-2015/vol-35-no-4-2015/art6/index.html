Abstract:

This book provides a comprehensive treatment of fixed point theory for Lipschitzian-type mappings, covering various aspects such as existence, uniqueness, and approximation of fixed points. It includes applications to differential equations, integral equations, and optimization problems.

Keywords:

[fixed point theory, Lipschitzian mappings, applications, differential equations, integral equations, optimization]
