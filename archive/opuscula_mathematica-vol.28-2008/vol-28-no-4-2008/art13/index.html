Remarks on the stability of some quadratic functional equations

Abstract:

Stability problems concerning the functional equations of the form \( f(2x + y) = 4f(x) + f(y) + f(x + y) - f(x - y) \), and \( f(2x + y) + f(2x - y) = 8f(x) + 2f(y) \) are investigated. We prove that if the norm of the difference between the LHS and the RHS of one of equations (1) or (2), calculated for a function \( g \) is say, dominated by a function \( \varphi \) in two variables having some standard properties then there exists a unique solution \( f \) of this equation and the norm of the difference between \( g \) and \( f \) is controlled by a function depending on \( \varphi \).

Keywords:
