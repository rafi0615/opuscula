On some subclasses of the family of Darboux Baire 1 functions

Abstract:

We introduce a subclass of the family of Darboux Baire 1 functions $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ modifying the Darboux property analogously as it was done by Z. Grande in [On a subclass of the family of Darboux functions, Colloq. Math. 17 (2009), 95-104], and replacing approximate continuity with $\mathcal{I}$-approximate continuity, i.e. continuity with respect to the $\mathcal{I}$-density topology. We prove that the family of all Darboux quasi-continuous functions from the first Baire class is a strongly porous set in the space $D\mathfrak{F}_1$ of Darboux Baire 1 functions, equipped with the supremum metric.

Keywords:

[Darboux property, strong Światkowski property, Baire property, I-approximate continuity, quasi-continuity.]
